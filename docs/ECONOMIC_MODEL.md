# OneDayCompany — Economic Model

## Purpose

This document describes the economic assumptions behind OneDayCompany.

Values are continuously updated using real production data.

---

# 1. Economic Principles

The objective is not to minimize AI cost.

The objective is to maximize value generated per euro spent.

Every AI operation should contribute to a measurable increase in business success.

---

# 2. AI Budget Engine

Users do not consume tokens.

Internally the platform manages AI Credits.

Each operation has a configurable internal cost.

Credits are independent from any specific LLM provider.

---

# 3. Free Usage

Every account receives:

- daily AI budget
- monthly AI budget

When exhausted, users may:

- wait for renewal
- subscribe to Pro

The maximum monthly AI exposure is therefore predictable.

---

# 4. Premium Usage

Premium plans increase available AI budget.

The user purchases productivity and business progress—not AI access.

---

---

# 8. AI Provider Strategy

Phase 1

Commercial APIs.

Phase 2

AI Router.

Phase 3

Hybrid infrastructure.

Premium reasoning models are used only where they materially improve user outcomes.

---

# 9. Financial KPIs

Track:

- CAC
- LTV
- Gross Margin
- AI Cost / Revenue
- Churn
- Conversion
- Burn Rate
- Runway

---

# 10. Scaling Strategy

Optimize in this order:

1. Product quality
2. Conversion
3. Retention
4. AI efficiency
5. Infrastructure

Never optimize infrastructure before product-market fit.
