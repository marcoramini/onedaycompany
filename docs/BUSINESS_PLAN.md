# OneDayCompany — Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

OneDayCompany is an AI-assisted Entrepreneur Operating System that helps individuals transform what they already know, love or have experienced into a real business.

The objective is not to generate business ideas, but to help users launch a real company, find their first customers and generate their first revenue through a guided workflow.

The AI is an enabling technology, not the product itself.

---

# 2. Mission

Help millions of people build businesses they are proud to create.

---

# 3. Vision

Artificial Intelligence should dramatically reduce the difficulty of becoming an entrepreneur.

Starting a company should become as accessible as creating a presentation or building a website.

---

# 4. Problem

Millions of people would like to work independently but:

- don't know where to start;
- don't believe they have valuable skills;
- become overwhelmed by business planning;
- never move from ideas to execution.

Existing AI tools generate content.

Very few guide users through building an actual business.

---

# 5. Solution

OneDayCompany guides users through a structured workflow that gradually creates:

- business direction;
- positioning;
- first offer;
- landing page;
- customer validation;
- outreach;
- first revenue.

Every interaction should increase the probability that a real business exists.

---

# 6. Target Market

Primary audience:

- individuals
- aspiring entrepreneurs
- professionals seeking independence
- freelancers
- creators
- career changers

Secondary audience:

- students
- side-project builders
- incubators
- entrepreneurship programs

---

# 7. Business Model

Subscription Software (SaaS).

Possible plans:

Free

- limited AI budget
- complete business creation experience
- daily renewal

Pro

- significantly higher AI budget
- faster progress
- premium features

Future

- Team
- Education
- Enterprise

---

# 8. Competitive Advantage

The competitive advantage is not the AI model.

It is:

- the workflow;
- the product methodology;
- structured business generation;
- iterative improvement;
- entrepreneur operating system.

---

# 9. AI Strategy

AI providers are replaceable.

The workflow is permanent.

The platform should progressively optimize cost while maintaining quality.

---

# 10. Pricing Philosophy

Users never purchase tokens.

Users purchase faster progress in building their company.

Pricing should remain stable regardless of AI provider.

---

# 11. Success Metrics

Examples:

- companies started
- companies launched
- first customers
- first revenue
- subscription conversion
- retention
- cost per active user
- gross margin

---

# 12. Growth Strategy

Phase 1

Validate product.

Phase 2

Reach product-market fit.

Phase 3

Scale through efficient AI infrastructure.

---

# 13. Risks

- AI costs
- low conversion
- weak retention
- provider dependency
- poor onboarding
- insufficient perceived value

Every major product decision should reduce at least one of these risks.
