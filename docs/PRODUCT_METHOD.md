# OneDayCompany — Product Method

## Purpose

The Product Method is the durable entrepreneurial sequence behind the product.
It remains independent from a specific model, provider or interface.

For every stage it answers:

1. What should feel more real after this stage?
2. What concrete output is created?
3. What user decision or evidence changes the company state?

## Method overview

```text
Begin with the person
  ↓
Shape one Company proposal
  ↓
Save a starting foundation, customers and offer
  ↓
Build visible launch assets
  ├── First offer
  ├── Logo and identity
  ├── Public presence
  └── Promotion
  ↓
Prepare customer operations
  ↓
Reach and serve first customers
  ↓
Learn, refine and grow
```

The sequence is not rigidly linear. The Workspace exposes parallel capabilities
while recommending the next lowest-friction action.

## Core principles

### Begin with what the user already has

Interests, experience, knowledge, imagination and preferred ways of working
are sufficient to begin. The product never evaluates entrepreneurial fitness.

### Create momentum before dependency

Early work should be autonomous, creative, reversible, inexpensive and visible.
A clear offer, logo direction, landing page and promotional material make the
company feel close to launch.

Permits, licenses, venue approval and partner responses appear only when
relevant and at the latest responsible moment before the action they block.

### Make the company visible early

Every company needs a public presence with one clear action. Its first format
depends on the company and may be a contact page, booking page, catalog,
portfolio, waitlist or product landing page.

### One screen, one objective

The Workspace may show the whole company, but each opened task or refinement
workflow concentrates on one explicit outcome.

### Preserve context and coherence

Mission, problem, solution, customers, offer, assets and execution state form
one company context. New work reuses that context instead of starting from a
blank prompt.

### Treat generated content as a proposal

AI output is not evidence and does not become accepted company state by itself.
The user remains the decision maker.

## Universal capabilities

The current application-owned capability set is:

1. company foundation;
2. first customers;
3. first offer;
4. brand identity;
5. public presence;
6. promotional launch;
7. customer operations.

The set is universal; implementation is company-specific. The application may
present capabilities in a different momentum-first order.

## Capability contract

Each capability contains:

```text
Objective
Current state
Ordered activities
Observable completion criteria
Structured outputs
Dependencies and impacts
Next action
```

Progress represents completed activities, not viewed screens or AI messages.

## Refinement loops

Any step or activity may be refined in a focused, contextual interaction.
Refinement must remain coherent with the whole company.

Required flow:

1. select one step or activity;
2. discuss the intended change;
3. generate a structured proposal;
4. identify direct and cascading impacts;
5. suggest a less disruptive alternative when useful;
6. review a complete diff;
7. explicitly accept or reject the proposal;
8. update affected state transactionally;
9. mark completed or published outputs for review when necessary.

The LLM cannot make direct or silent changes.

## Progress states

The durable model should distinguish at least:

- `not_started` — no usable result exists;
- `draft` — a starting result exists;
- `in_progress` — activities are underway;
- `ready` — the capability is usable for the next launch action;
- `published` or `active` — customers can use it;
- `needs_review` — an accepted upstream change may have invalidated it;
- `updating` — an accepted revision is being implemented.

## Evidence and growth

Visual assets create momentum but do not prove demand. After launch, the method
must increasingly privilege real behavior:

```text
Attention → Contact → Time commitment → Booking or trial
→ Payment → Repeat purchase or referral
```

Growth decisions should use this evidence rather than generated confidence.

## Guardrails

The method must not:

- behave like a generic chatbot;
- overwhelm the user with bureaucracy at the beginning;
- present hypotheses as validated facts;
- force complex software before a simple public presence;
- overwrite accepted or published work silently;
- measure progress through screen views;
- generate many options when one decision is required;
- let an AI provider define the durable workflow.
