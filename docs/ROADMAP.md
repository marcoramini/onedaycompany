## Backlog principles

A backlog item should be prioritized only when it:

- improves the probability of launching a business;
- removes a major point of friction;
- improves learning from real users;
- supports a current milestone;
- reduces material technical risk.

Avoid adding features simply because they are common in SaaS products.
